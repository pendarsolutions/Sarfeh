Sarfeh 0.6.1 adds HostIran, the first provider that asked to be listed.

## Added

- **HostIran's cloud servers**: 14 plans, from General Purpose 1 (1 core, 1 GB memory, 20 GB disk) to Memory
  Optimized 6 (32 cores, 128 GB, 240 GB), from the plan table HostIran sent on 25 November 2026. They're in the
  catalog, and on the Catalog page.

  HostIran hasn't published its traffic terms yet (what's included, or what a GB costs), so the engine doesn't price
  its plans exactly, and doesn't suggest them. The [price page](https://pendarsolutions.ir/sarfeh/prices/) says so
  beside HostIran. Once the terms are out, they reach your installation through Sarfeh's price service, without
  another release.

## Changed

- The price page says when a provider sells a plan of the size asked for but hasn't published a term its price
  depends on, instead of saying it has nothing that size.
- This release carries the price catalog of 25 November 2026, with every provider read that day or the day before.

## Upgrade

```bash
sudo sarfeh upgrade
```

It backs up first, then moves to 0.6.1 and keeps your settings and data. From 0.6.0, that's all. From an older
version, read the [0.6.0 notes](https://github.com/{{REPO}}/releases/tag/v0.6.0) first: prices now come from Sarfeh's
price service.

The full list of changes is in the [changelog](https://github.com/{{REPO}}/blob/v{{VERSION}}/CHANGELOG.md).
